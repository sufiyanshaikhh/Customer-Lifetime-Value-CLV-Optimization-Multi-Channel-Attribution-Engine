"Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Optimization & Multi-Channel Attribution Engine"

The Business Context: 
A fast-growing B2C e-commerce platform invests heavily in digital acquisition channels such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, Influencer Marketing, Email Campaigns, and Organic Search to drive customer growth. While acquisition volume continues to increase, profitability does not improve at the same pace.

The organization faces two strategic challenges:
-It treats every acquired customer as equally valuable, leading to inefficient retention and marketing investments.
-It relies on a last-click attribution model that overcredits the final interaction while ignoring the contribution of earlier marketing touchpoints, resulting in suboptimal budget allocation.

Business Analytics Framework:
Phase 01: Customer Value Optimization (Analytical Stage)
Business Objective: Maximize the long-term value of every acquired customer by understanding their behavior, predicting future value, and personalizing engagement.
01: Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Prediction
Purpose: Estimate the future revenue or profit a customer is expected to generate.
Stakeholders: Marketing,CRM,Finance
02: Customer Segmentation
Purpose: Group customers based on value and behavior.
Possible segments: High-value loyal customers,Frequent buyers,New customers,Discount seekers,At-risk customers,Churned customers.
Stakeholders: Marketing,CRM
03: Churn Prediction
Purpose: Predict customers likely to stop purchasing.
Stakeholders: CRM,Customer Success
04: Customer Health Score (Business KPI)
Possible identifiers: Purchase frequency,Recency,Average order value,Churn probability,Engagement,Returns
05: Customer Journey Analysis
Purpose: How customers behave after acquisition.

Success Metrics:
Increase Average Customer Lifetime Value,Improve Customer Retention Rate,Reduce Customer Churn Rate,Increase Repeat
Purchase Rate,Increase Average Order Value

Phase 02: Marketing Effectiveness Optimization (Analytical Stage)
Business Objective: Measure how marketing channels contribute to revenue and optimize budget allocation.
01: Multi-Touch Attribution
Purpose: Replace Last Click Attribution.
Possible Models: Linear,Time Decay,Position-Based,Markov Chain (advanced)
Stakeholders: Performance Marketing
02: Channel Performance Analysis
Purpose: Evaluate every acquisition channel.
Metrics: Revenue,Customers acquired,Conversion Rate,Repeat Purchases,CLV,CAC,ROAS
Stakeholder: Finance
03: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Analysis
Purpose: Understand acquisition efficiency.
04: CLV : CAC Ratio (Business Metric)
Purpose: Identify which channel produces the highest lifetime value relative to acquisition cost.
05: Marketing ROI Analysis
Purpose: Measure actual profitability.
06: Budget Optimization Simulator
Purpose: shift from descriptive analytics to decision support

Success Metrics: 
Increase ROAS,Reduce Customer Acquisition Cost,Improve CLV:CAC Ratio,Increase Marketing ROI,Improve Attribution Accuracy

Phase 03: Executive Dashboard (Decision Intelligence) 
Objective: Provide executives with a unified, real-time view of customer health, marketing performance, and business profitability to support strategic decision-making.
Stakeholders: CEO / CMO
Dashboard components: 
01: Executive KPIs
Revenue,Profit,Marketing Spend,CAC,CLV,CLV:CAC Ratio,ROAS,Retention Rate,Churn Rate
02: Customer Analytics
CLV distribution,Customer segments,Churn risk,Customer health score,Cohort retention
03: Marketing Analytics
Attribution by channel,Campaign ROI,Channel contribution,Budget allocation, Funnel analysis

Success Metrics:
Reduce decision-making time,Increase executive visibility,Improve campaign monitoring,Improve KPI accessibility

Expected Business Impact:
By implementing the proposed analytical framework, the organization aims to:
Improve customer retention through proactive churn intervention.
Personalize marketing based on customer value and behavior.
Allocate marketing budgets using data-driven attribution insights.
Increase marketing profitability through optimized channel investment.
Provide executives with actionable decision intelligence through a centralized dashboard.
